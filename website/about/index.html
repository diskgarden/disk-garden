About DiskGarden

A free disk-space map for the Mac.

Running out of space on a Mac usually ends in guesswork: deleting a few big videos, emptying Downloads, hoping it was enough. DiskGarden replaces the guesswork with a picture. It reads your whole disk in seconds and draws it as a bloom, where every folder is a petal as wide as the space it takes.

It is a native Mac app — made for macOS, in the system's own look, in light and dark — and it is careful by design: nothing is deleted until you've reviewed it in the Waste bin, and the parts of the system macOS needs are off limits.

DiskGarden is free. There is no account to create and no ads.
