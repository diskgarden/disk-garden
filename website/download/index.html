Free for Mac. One .dmg, no installer wizard, no account. Drag it to Applications and you're scanning in under a minute.

Install in three steps

1

Open the .dmg

Double-click DiskGarden-1.0.4.dmg in your Downloads folder. A window with the app and an Applications shortcut appears.

2

Drag to Applications

Drop the DiskGarden icon onto the Applications folder, then eject the disk image. That's the whole install.

3

Open and scan

macOS asks once whether to open an app from the internet. To map hidden space too, allow DiskGarden under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access.
